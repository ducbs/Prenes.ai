New rules create demand. Under the Cybersecurity Law 2025 and Decree 332/2026/NĐ-CP, organizations need cybersecurity services from licensed providers. Prenes.ai partners can offer them a compliant answer.

Two Waysto Partner

Whether or not you hold a cybersecurity service license, there is a path for you.

Path 1

Licensed Service Partner

For system integrators that hold a cybersecurity service license in Vietnam.

  • Buy services and platform subscriptions directly from Prenes.ai
  • Contract directly with your customers
  • Sell hardware with local warranty
  • Highest partner pricing tier
Prenes.ai→You→Customer
Path 2

Reseller Partner

For integrators and IT providers without a cybersecurity service license.

  • Sell through our authorized distributor in Vietnam
  • The distributor holds the service contract; you keep the relationship
  • Hardware can be bought directly from Prenes.ai
  • Move to Path 1 once you are licensed
Prenes.ai→Distributor→You→Customer
