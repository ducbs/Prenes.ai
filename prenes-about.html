WhoWe Are

Prenes.ai helps organizations answer two questions: where are we exposed, and is someone attacking us right now? We combine exposure management, 24/7 monitoring and purpose-built hardware so customers can see their risk early and act on threats in time.

Our first market is Vietnam. Services are delivered together with strategic partners in Vietnam that hold all required licenses, and our monitoring platform is hosted in the country so monitoring data stays local.

OurPhilosophy

Mission

Four PrinciplesWe Work By

Licensed delivery

We only offer what the law allows, delivered in Vietnam with partners that hold the required licenses.

Data stays local

Our monitoring platform is hosted in a data center in Vietnam, one isolated tenant per customer.

Measured, not promised

Service levels are agreed after a pilot and reported every month — not claimed in advance.

You stay in control

We detect, assess and recommend. Changes to your systems are always made by your team.
