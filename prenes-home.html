HowIt Works

From your network to a partner analyst — in four steps.

01

Copy & collect

TAP and Packet Broker copy traffic; the Sensor Platform gathers logs and pre-filters at your site.

02

Send securely

Encrypted, outbound-only transfer. An optional AI server analyses sensitive data on-premises.

03

Analyze on the platform

Hosted in Canada / U.S. across two regions, each customer in an isolated tenant.

04

Partner responds

Your MSSP partner triages, investigates and responds under its contract with you.
