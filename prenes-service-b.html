Alerts AreNot Protection

Banks and insurers generate millions of events a day. Knowing which ones matter — at any hour — takes an integrated platform and people.

01

Too many alerts

Firewalls, servers and endpoints produce thousands of events a day. The real attack hides in the noise.

02

Attacks happen after hours

Ransomware operators strike at night, on weekends and on public holidays.

03

Tools don't talk to each other

Data centers, branches, mobile apps and cloud spread across separate consoles.
