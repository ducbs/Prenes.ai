Alerts AreNot Protection

Security tools generate signals. Knowing which ones matter — at any hour — takes people.

01

Too many alerts

Firewalls, servers and endpoints produce thousands of events a day. The real attack hides in the noise.

02

Attacks happen after hours

Ransomware operators strike at night, on weekends and on public holidays.

03

A 24/7 team is hard to build

Hiring and keeping analysts for round-the-clock shifts is slow and expensive.

We Detect.

A PlatformHosted in Vietnam

A shared, multi-tenant monitoring platform in a Vietnamese data center — each customer in its own tenant, with data kept separate.

On-site

The Sensor Box

A Prenes.ai Sensor Platform (or a refurbished server in early deployments) collects and pre-filters data at your site. It is rented as part of your package.

  • Hardened OS, encrypted disk, dedicated admin account
  • Outbound connection only — no inbound ports opened
  • Health alerts for connectivity, disk and hardware
  • Data wiped and box collected when the contract ends
Platform security

How We Protect Your Data

A security provider holds data from many customers, so we apply strict controls to our own environment.

  • Least-privilege access: analysts see only assigned customers
  • Multi-factor authentication and full access logging
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Regular independent testing and a tested recovery plan
