Prenes.aiSensor Platform

Prenes.ai Sensor Platform is sensor and collector software delivered on standard server hardware. Installed at your site, it gathers and pre-filters logs and network data, then sends them securely to the Prenes.ai platform. It is the standard collection unit in Professional and Enterprise.

Request a quote
FunctionCollect · pre-filter · forward
ConnectionOutbound only, encrypted
SoftwareSensor & collector preloaded
HardwareStandard server, sized to your site

KeyFeatures

Preloaded software

Sensor and collector installed and configured before shipping.

Hardened by default

Hardened operating system, encrypted disk and a dedicated admin account.

Outbound only

Connects out to the monitoring platform over Internet, VPN or a private line — no inbound ports.

Health alerts

Alerts for connectivity, disk and hardware so gaps in collection are noticed.

Built for the platform

Feeds the platform; your MSSP partner investigates and responds with your approval.

Remote management

Configuration and software updates pushed from the platform.

Origin& Compliance

Truthful origin

Country of origin is declared where each unit is actually made, and labels always match the documents. Canada / U.S. assembly with traceable records supports tenders that require G7 origin; an Asian-origin variant is available where accepted, labeled with its true origin.

Export controls

Every order is screened against Canadian and U.S. export controls and sanctions. Partners handle any local product licensing in their country.

Supply-chain commitments

No hidden code or backdoors, right to inspect, defined firmware maintenance and a second-source plan.
