Kept in Vietnam

Monitoring data stays on the platform in Vietnam and is accessed by authorized operations staff in Vietnam.

Separated by tenant

Each customer has its own space on the platform; data is never mixed between customers.

Agreed in writing

A data processing agreement sets storage, access and deletion before the service starts.

AtYour Site

Sensor box

Secure by design

A Prenes.ai Sensor Platform (or a refurbished server in early deployments) collects data locally. It is a device in your network, so it is locked down.

  • Hardened OS and encrypted disk
  • Dedicated admin account
  • Outbound connection only — no inbound ports
  • Standard configuration, tested before delivery
Connectivity & lifecycle

Reliable and managed

We watch the health of every box and replace it on a regular cycle.

  • Connects over Internet, VPN or leased line
  • Alerts for lost connection, full disk or hardware faults
  • Spare units kept ready for replacement
  • Wiped and collected when the contract ends

Detection &
Recommendation Mode

The platform is configured to detect and recommend. Automatic isolation, blocking and patching controlled by Prenes.ai are switched off on customer systems.

Change control

  • Rule and scan changes reviewed and approved
  • Every change has a rollback plan
  • Changes logged

Three environments

  • Production for customer service
  • Testing for rules and integrations
  • Lab for training — never real customer data

SecuringPrenes.ai Itself

A provider holding data from many customers is an attractive target. These controls protect it.

Least privilege

Analysts access only assigned customers; privileged access managed and every access logged.

MFA & vaulting

Multi-factor authentication for admin accounts; API keys and scan credentials in a secure vault.

Separate admin network

The operations network is isolated from office networks, with layered authentication.

Independent testing

Regular penetration tests and configuration reviews of our own infrastructure.

National monitoring. The platform is being designed to support the connection with the national cybersecurity monitoring system required of monitoring service providers under Decree 332/2026/NĐ-CP.
